It starts at the top

You need the best possible thinkers and action takers on your board and executive team. If they are not walking the talk, your organisation will not be either.

No one is a perfect thinker. We are all human. The frailties that produce poor quality thinking catch everyone, and the more senior you are, the harder they become to avoid. Fewer people challenge you openly. Information arrives faster than it can be considered. The diary fills with whatever is urgent.

The agenda has never asked more of judgement

AI, geopolitics, climate change, cyber, workforce change and public trust no longer sit on your agenda as separate items. They collide inside the same decisions, and a choice that looks right in one part of the organisation can create exposure in another.

The visible agenda is the topics. The real work underneath is judgement. Growth or resilience. Speed or control. Efficiency or capability. That judgement is only as good as the thinking behind it.

Five questions worth sitting with

01

How many of your top team could describe the future you are building in the same words?

02

Think about your last three significant decisions. In how many did somebody say out loud that they disagreed?

03

Where does this team over-think, and where does it under-think?

04

Are your leaders using AI to buy back time and augment their judgement, or to outsource their thinking?

05

Hand on heart, can every member of your board and executive team say they are being the best possible thinker and action taker?

How we could help,
asked as questions

These are the six things we can empower your top team with. The conversations, decisions and actions stay yours. Pick the one or two that would make the biggest difference here. That is where we would start.

•

A shared language. Could your team name what great thinking looks like here, and what poor thinking looks like, well enough to ask for one and name the other?

•

What matters most. How aligned are you on the most important thinking, conversations, decisions and actions?

•

Time and energy. What protects the thinking your biggest decisions need, and where could AI buy back time so more of it goes on what matters most?

•

The atmosphere you create. What would your meetings produce if the room drew the best thinking out of everyone, healthy challenge included?

•

Knowing yourself as a thinker. How well does each of you know your own strengths, patterns and traps, including how you work with AI?

•

Being a thinking partner, with others and AI. What would your biggest decisions look like if you were all being the best possible thinking partners to each other, and using AI to augment your judgement rather than outsource it?
